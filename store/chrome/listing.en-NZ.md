- Category: `Productivity`

## Detailed description

Kiwi Job Search Enhancer helps New Zealand job seekers reduce noise in the
search results they already opened.

Use personal keyword, company and available category filters; dismiss jobs you
do not want to see again; restore them later; and keep results within a
conservative straight-line distance from a commute origin you choose.

Key features:

- Filters only job cards already displayed on supported result pages.
- Adds local Seen, approximate-distance and Not interested controls.
- Keeps rules, commute information and job history on the device.
- Does not request listing APIs, automatically paginate, run analytics or show ads.
- Supports English (New Zealand) and Simplified Chinese.

The supported site is SEEK New Zealand. This independent extension is not
affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by SEEK.
Site layouts can change; when a page cannot be parsed safely, filtering fails
open and leaves jobs visible.

## Single purpose

Enhance supported New Zealand job-search result pages with user-controlled,
local-first filtering and job-status tools.

## Reviewer testing instructions

1. Open a supported SEEK New Zealand result page.
2. Select the extension toolbar icon to open the Side Panel.
3. Under Filters, add a keyword rule for a word visible in a job title.
4. Confirm matching cards disappear immediately and can be revealed from Overview.
5. Select Not interested on another card, then restore it from Hidden.
6. Under Commute, choose a known New Zealand place and save a maximum distance.
7. Geolocation is optional to the workflow and is only invoked by selecting
   “Use my current location”.
