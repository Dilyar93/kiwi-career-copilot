Independent browser extension · version 0.8.0

Keep the job results you want to see.

Kiwi Job Search Enhancer adds negative keyword, category, company and conservative distance filters to job cards already visible on supported New Zealand job-search pages.

Local-first by design. Rules, job history and commute information stay on your device; the extension has no operator server, analytics or advertising.

Local-first by design

Personal filters

Exclude unwanted results, dismiss individual jobs, restore them later and mark viewed cards.

Conservative distance

Use one local commute origin. Unknown, remote and unsafe-to-resolve locations stay visible.

No job scraping

The extension processes only job cards on the page you opened. It does not fetch listings, open detail pages or advance pagination.

English and Chinese

English (New Zealand) is the runtime default. Simplified Chinese is an explicit user choice.

Supported site

SEEK New Zealand on desktop Chromium browsers. This independent project is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by SEEK.
